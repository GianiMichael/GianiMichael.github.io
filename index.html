U.S. Net Metering Analysis, 2015–2024

Problem
No easy way to see how U.S. commercial solar adoption and net metering capacity have evolved state-by-state over the last decade.
Approach
Cleaned ten years of EIA Form 861 data in Python, modeled it in Power BI, and published it as an interactive Tableau dashboard.
Result
A public, interactive view of solar capacity growth and commercial adoption trends across all 50 states.

DERM ROI Calculator

Problem
Commercial and industrial customers with flexible/curtailable load have no easy way to see what enrolling in a demand response program is actually worth, since the economics span multiple revenue streams with different rules.
Approach
Built an interactive scenario model in Python, calculating gross value across 4CP avoidance, ancillary services, and real-time price spike avoidance for the ERCOT market, then published it as a live web dashboard where adjusting curtailable capacity, forecast accuracy, and market conditions recalculates every chart and stat using the same formulas as the underlying engine.
Result
A shareable tool that turns an abstract demand response pitch into a concrete, adjustable proposal a customer can see the numbers on in real time.
  • Python
  • Demand Response
  • ERCOT
